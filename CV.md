### CASMI 2026: molecular identification from mass spectrometry
**Independent data science / machine learning project** · 2026, in progress · [Competition](https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/enveda-CASMI26-molecule-id-mass-spectra)

An end-to-end pipeline for the Enveda CASMI 2026 Kaggle competition: infer unknown 2D molecular structures from tandem mass spectra and return ranked candidate structures as SMILES. The approach is retrieval-first and validation-first, with reproducible evidence behind every experiment and model comparison.

- Training corpus of about 2.5 million MS/MS spectra and hundreds of thousands of molecular structures.
- Built data-processing, candidate-retrieval, molecular-feature and ranking workflows in Python, pandas, scikit-learn and RDKit.
- Designed the evaluation: MRR@25, structure-disjoint splits, leakage controls and independently recomputed metrics.
- Protected research integrity with Git versioning, automated tests, CI, experiment provenance and fail-closed validation.
- Used GPU compute where justified, and built live experiment visualisation and recording that turns results into educational scientific stories.
